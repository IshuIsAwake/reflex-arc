# The pitch, in eight beats

How to bring somebody into this from nothing — a judge, a teammate, anyone who was not in the room.
Written 2026-08-24 for an ideation session that never ran, and kept because the running order is
still the best version of the pitch we have.

**People who shape a thing do not need to be assigned to it.** Ownership in
[`../work_division.md`](../work_division.md) is a starting point, and this is how someone gets
brought far enough in to redraw it.

## The shape: bottom-up, eight beats, the project named only at beat 6

1. Hand on a hot stove.
2. Mars at 3–22 minutes each way — so who stops the rover driving into a hole?
3. Earth's half of the loop is a room full of PhDs, and was never automated.
4. Why an LLM cannot drive and RL cannot decide.
5. A floor map, six wheels, no brain on board.
6. **Delay the link twenty seconds and it keeps going.**
7. The map is fogged, so the job is deciding what is worth looking at.
8. And it says why, in English.

## Notes on running it

- **Beat 4 is the transcript, not an argument.** [`00-raw-transcript.md`](00-raw-transcript.md)
  262–345 — the model finds the Seal, derives a farm route, is told to go get it, and has no hands.
  Show it rather than describing it.
- **The target is "what if we…", not "wow."** Awe that is not understood produces politeness, and a
  finished design can only be delegated, not joined.
- **Each half must be load-bearing for the other**, or each side files the other under *not my part*
  and stops listening. The latency demo works *because* the rover has no brain; the learned policy
  is necessary *because* what the ground costs to cross can only be measured off the real machine.
- **Leave the open questions genuinely open.** Whatever is undecided when the room fills up should
  still be undecided when it empties, if the people in it did not decide it.
- Say the hardcoded-table risk out loud. It is what makes this a problem rather than a task.
