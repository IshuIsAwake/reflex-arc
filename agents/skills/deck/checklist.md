# Deck checklist

Check a deck's slide text, and its rendered slides if you can see them, against `SKILL.md` and the repo's own rules.
Report problems; don't fix them while checking.

## What to read
1. What you were given: the slide text, a PDF export, or the slide images.
2. The repo's rules (`agents/repo_rules.md`, `agents/this_repo.md`), and the brief or template the deck answers if
   there is one. Where they differ from `SKILL.md`, the repo wins.
3. `SKILL.md`.

## What to ask, in this order
1. Would someone who isn't technical follow it? Jargon, an undefined term, a number with no comparison.
2. Is every number backed? A number with no output, table, file or source behind it, an estimate not marked as one,
   a claim of "first" or "only".
3. Is it the idea or the demo? The demo pitched in place of the idea, feasibility argued from the demo.
4. Text: more than one idea on a slide, a point over two lines (about 15 words), text under an image over three
   sentences, a point that reads like a spoken script, a block packing several facts that should be points or a
   small table, a number said twice on one slide.
5. Shape and look: headings out of the template's order, an image without a subheading, a chart whose title doesn't
   state its finding, text cut off, overlapping or too small to read.

## Report
- Keep the order above. One line per problem: the slide number and the point's first few words, what is wrong, then
  the shorter or plainer version.
- Mark a judgment call as one, and say why.
- End with the number of problems, or "Nothing found". No praise and no summary of the deck.
